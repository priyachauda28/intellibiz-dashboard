Key insights & possible actions

Observations based on the full dataset
1

May was the revenue peak. €1,762.46, about 19.3% of delivered revenue. Review May’s campaign, product and channel mix to identify repeatable drivers.

2

South was the largest region. €3,124.60 (34.2% of delivered revenue). Compare its winning products and channel mix with lower-performing regions.

3

Electronics led category sales. €2,891.25 (31.6% of delivered revenue); Wireless Earbuds alone contributed €1,605.80. Check stock availability and test targeted cross-sell bundles.

4

Customer ratings show a focused opportunity. Checkout averaged 3.00 (n=2) and Price 3.25 (n=4). Review checkout friction and perceived value, while treating these small topic samples cautiously.

Data quality & limitations

  • Standardised Region: trimmed whitespace and title-cased values; “ south ” → “South”.
  • Standardised Channel: “website” → “Website”.
  • Standardised product name: merged “Wireless ear buds” into “Wireless Earbuds”.
  • Flagged, not imputed: ORD-0104 has a blank Discount_Pct (the recorded revenue is retained); FB-011, FB-015 and FB-033 have rating 1 alongside positive comments. These records are highlighted for review rather than silently changed.
  • Feedback limitation: only 40 responses are available. Topic-level samples are small and may not represent the full customer base; feedback has no region/channel fields and is therefore not filterable with orders.
  • KPI scope: Revenue, Orders and Average Order Value use Delivered orders. Return Rate uses Returned orders divided by all orders in the current order-filter selection. Feedback rating uses all 40 responses.
